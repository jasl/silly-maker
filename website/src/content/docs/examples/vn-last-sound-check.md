**One Last Sound Check** is SillyMaker's maintained flagship Reference Product:
an original, complete compact visual novel built from the tracked starter and
reviewed as a real product rather than a vertical slice.

[Play the VN in this site build](../../play/vn-last-sound-check/)

## Product denominator

- 110 unique visible text entries; either route exposes 82;
- two named characters, two authored scenes, one material choice, two distinct
  routes, and two endings;
- complete Chinese and English text packs, final visual and audio assets;
- the engine-maintained VN Player, Save/recovery, History, Back/Forward, Auto,
  skip-read, responsive input, and accessibility behavior;
- one shared Inspector/CAS path used for human and Agent authoring handoff.

The Browser player is bundled into this publishable site artifact at
`/play/vn-last-sound-check/`. Deno Desktop remains a supported development target;
this page does not claim a separately deployed native release.

## Evidence boundary

Independent product and engine reviews passed, and the starter required no
additional change. A representative real current-low-end qualification was not
performed: the owner removed that unavailable device gate from this product's
completion criteria. This is not evidence of low-end device support.
