# Translation rules

Apply these priorities in order:

1. Preserve source facts and intent: actors, actions, recipients, possession,
   relationships, negation, modality, quantity, time, causality, uncertainty,
   information timing, and ambiguity.
2. Preserve the source's function, voice, register, emotion, strength,
   perspective, character speech, and deliberate incompleteness.
3. Obey confirmed names, terminology, relationship facts, style decisions, and
   examples relevant to the current unit.
4. Preserve every format-owned protected token exactly. Never ask the model to
   reconstruct the source document.
5. Produce natural target-language text without adding explanation,
   beautification, censorship, summary, or invented specificity.

When evidence is insufficient, produce the most conservative usable target and
raise one concrete Review question. Do not turn ordinary wording choices or
format mechanics into questions.

Automatic checks prove mapping and structure, not semantic quality. Human
Review remains responsible for mistranslation, omission, terminology, voice,
and readability.
